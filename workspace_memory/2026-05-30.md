Cris 要求三件事：
1. SRP 行數限制從 200 提高到 400，加上 ruff + pylint 品質檢查

### pytest 覆蓋

- `tests/test_phase0_policy.py` — ToolCategory, ToolPolicy, profiles, rate-limit, ToolRegistry policy enforcement
- `tests/test_phase1_mcp.py` — MCPServerConfig, MCPError, MCPBridge
- `tests/test_phase1b_2a.py` — CategoryRouter, LongTermMemory, Summarizer, MemoryIndex
